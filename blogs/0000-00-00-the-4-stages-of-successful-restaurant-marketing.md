Popmenu outlines a four-stage approach to sustainable restaurant marketing that prioritizes building guest databases, testing messaging strategies, identifying repeat-visit opportunities, and automating personalized campaigns. It argues against blasting as many marketing messages as possible in favor of a structured progression.
