The post addresses why independent restaurant operators hesitate to email guests and how to overcome that fear, noting that guests who share contact info actually want to hear from the business. It cites marketing manager Mitchell Abes and an example where a single "we miss you" email generated over $52,500 in orders, and recommends AI tools and automated messages to streamline content creation.
