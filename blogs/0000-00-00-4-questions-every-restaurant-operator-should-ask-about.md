Popmenu calls catering one of the highest-margin revenue streams a restaurant can have and outlines four questions operators should address: why to display prices online, which catering types to offer based on location, the value of holiday catering, and how to promote catering services. It emphasizes using existing customer touchpoints to grow the channel.
